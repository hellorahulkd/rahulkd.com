The idea

imagine spending a sunday somewhere new. good people, fresh air, a view you didn't know existed.

Why

because travel changes people. meeting someone from a different country, seeing a place you've never been, sharing a meal with a stranger. these moments open your mind in ways nothing else can.

How

small group day trips and weekend escapes from sydney. small enough that you actually meet everyone, big enough to feel like an adventure. always led personally, never a faceless agency.

What

affordable, well-organised day trips for uni students 19–26, domestic and international. starting with port stephens and growing from there.

Where it started

port stephens. one van. somewhere beautiful.

the first trip, a sunday in may. this is roughly what a passport people day looks like: an early start, a lot of sand, and the kind of day you're still talking about next week.

The story

hi, i'm rahul. i came to sydney as an international student. growing up i was a shy, introverted kid, and travel is what changed that. meeting people from everywhere, learning about their worlds, seeing places i never imagined. it turned me into someone who genuinely loves bringing people together.

sydney is one of the most international cities on earth, full of students from every corner of the globe. and yet most weekends, we all end up in the same routine. passport people is my way of changing that, one sunday at a time.

if you want to know more about me, i'm at rahulkd.com.
