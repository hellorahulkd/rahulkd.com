The questions we get

Asked most weeks
Do I have to pay before I get my refund?+

No. For most individual and sole trader returns the fee can come out of the refund, so there's nothing out of pocket on the day.

I haven't lodged since 2021. Am I in trouble?+

Almost certainly not as much as you think. The ATO is far more interested in people who come forward than people who hide. We'll work out what's outstanding and lodge it in order.

What can I actually claim as a tradie?+

Tools, protective gear, the ute, fuel and logbook, phone and internet apportioned to work, union fees, and travel between sites. Not the drive from home to your first job — that one catches everybody.

Do you do my BAS as well?+

Yes, quarterly from $130. If your books are a mess we'll quote bookkeeping separately rather than bury it in the BAS fee.

Can we do it all without me coming in?+

Easily. Most of our clients send photos and sign electronically. The office is there if you'd rather sit down with someone.
