About Me

Who's this guy?

I picked up a camera about ten years ago and never really put it down. Photography became video, video became storytelling, and here we are.

I landed in Sydney from Nepal at 19 and knew roughly nobody. My first job here was at KFC. These days it's campaign videos with millions of views between them, 30+ clients brought into a firm on a $0 ad budget, and 3,000+ students who somehow elected me to represent them.

Point me at either side of the camera and I'll be useful — I'll write it, shoot it, present it, cut it, then overthink the thumbnail. I'd rather earn your audience's trust slowly than borrow their attention for an afternoon.
