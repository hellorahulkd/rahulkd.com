Print me

  1. Print A4, landscape, double-sided, scale 100% — not “fit to page”, or the QR shrinks.
  2. Fold once down the middle. Sheet 1 is the outside, sheet 2 is the inside.
  3. If the inside comes out upside-down, reprint and switch the duplex setting between “flip on short edge” and “flip on long edge”. Drivers disagree about which is which on landscape pages — short edge is the one to try first.
  4. Print shop version: hand them the PDF and ask for A4, 170–250gsm silk, double sided, full bleed, folded once to A5. They’ll sort the rest.
  5. Home printer? You’ll get a thin white edge around the orange. Still works fine.
